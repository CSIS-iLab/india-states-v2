**States Weekly Newsletter**

**July 22, 2026**

**Big News: Telangana** introduces a **New Employees Health Scheme (NEHS); Delhi** approves a **Delhi Start-up and Incubation Policy**; **West Bengal** approves the **West Bengal Incentive Scheme (WBIS) 2026 for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**; **Telangana** releases **Draft Core Urban Region (Integrated Governance) Bill;** and more.

**Health**

**Haryana** increases the **monthly income ceiling of medical reimbursement for dependents from \$36.24 to \$93.20 (INR 3,500 to INR 9,000)**. The increase applies to dependents of government employees and pensioners.

**Source:** [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/haryana-government-raises-dependents-income-limit-for-medical-reimbursement/)

**In detail:** [Government notification](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s31177967c7957072da3dc1db4ceb30e7a/uploads/2026/07/20260714774422595.pdf)

**Telangana** introduces a **New Employees Health Scheme (NEHS).** The scheme covers state government employees, pensioners, and their eligible family members, offering cashless treatment across a network of 886 private and 114 government hospitals.

**Source:** [Business world](https://www.bwhealthcareworld.com/article/telangana-unveils-new-employees-health-scheme-covering-17-88-lakh-beneficiaries-615357)

**In detail:** [Telangana Health Scheme](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:d7bc5625-8628-44d1-81de-dc1b0ce9c50a)

**Climate Change and Energy**

**Himachal Pradesh** issues **Draft** **HPERC (Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic Grid Interactive System) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026.** The regulations, issued by the Himachal Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (HPERC), aim to simplify rooftop solar approval procedures and installation.

**Source:** [Construction World](https://www.constructionworld.in/policy-updates-and-economic-news/hperc-proposes-digital-reforms-to-simplify-rooftop-solar-connections/94717)

**In detail:** [HPERC (Rooftop Solar Photovoltaic Grid Interactive System) (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026](https://hperc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/drooftopsolar4-26.pdf)

**Finance**

Nothing critical to report this week.

**Industrial Policy and Business Regulations**

**Delhi** approves a **Delhi Start-up and Incubation Policy**. Under this policy, more than \$41.42 million (INR 400 crore) will be invested over the next 5 years devoted to innovation, research, and entrepreneurship. In its primary phase, it will be implemented in 11 universities, 13 government-aided colleges, polytechnic institutions, Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) and government-run schools.

**Source:** [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/slug-lite/government-approves-delhi-start-up-and-incubation-policy)

**West Bengal** approves the **West Bengal Incentive Scheme (WBIS) 2026 for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**. The scheme, in effect from October 1, 2026, will promote industrial growth, employment generation and balanced regional development. It will remain operational for 5 years until September 30, 2031.

**Source:** [KNN](https://knnindia.co.in/news/newsdetails/msme/west-bengal-approves-wbis-2026-msme-incentive-scheme-with-interest-and-power-subsidies)

**Chhattisgarh** passes the **Chhattisgarh Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026.** The legislation adopts a statutory risk-based approval system aimed at reducing bureaucratic delays, cutting compliance costs, and accelerating investments, particularly for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/raipur/chhattisgarh-passes-ease-of-doing-business-law-msmes-to-get-self-certification-deemed-approvals/articleshow/132444179.cms)

**Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations**

**Punjab** replaces **35 complex revenue terms** **with easy-to-understand Punjabi words**. In simplifying the language used in property registration documents, it aims at making land and property transactions more accessible to the public.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/punjab-simplifies-property-registration-language-replaces-35-complex-revenue-terms/articleshow/132493935.cms)

**Infrastructure and Governance**

**Bihar** approves **Draft** **Gram Panchayat Taxes, Rates, and Fees Rules, 2026.** The decision formally authorizes Gram Panchayats (rural governing bodies) to impose and collect holding taxes and various other fees in rural areas.

**Source:** [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/india/bihar-village-holding-tax-rural-property-samrat-choudhary-government-10790096/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/1/Smart%20City/Cabinet%20Decisions/Cabinet%20Press15.pdf)

**Delhi** approves the **Delhi Right of Citizens to Time-Bound and Easy Delivery of Services Bill, 2026**. It replaces an earlier 2011 law with a modern, technology-driven legal framework for a "citizen-centric" governance model.

**Source:** [Financial World](https://www.thefinancialworld.com/delhi-cabinet-clears-right-to-time-bound-services-bill/)

**Karnataka** releases **Draft Karnataka Apartment (Ownership and Management) Bill, 2026 (KAOMA)**. The legislation aims to replace laws over five decades old with a unified framework to strengthen owners' rights, improve governance, and establish a clear mechanism for redeveloping ageing residential complexes.

**Source:** [PropTech](https://www.aurumproptech.in/pulse/media/karnataka-draft-apartment-bill-2026-key-proposals-explained)

**In detail:** [Karnataka Apartment (Ownership and Management) Bill, 2026](https://udd.karnataka.gov.in/uploads/media_to_upload1784100708.pdf)

**Maharashtra** approves an **Asset Monetization Policy.** It aims to generate income sources through assets for municipal corporations and municipal councils.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/mumbai/maharashtra-cabinet-approves-asset-monetisation-policy-for-civic-bodies/articleshow/132395020.cms)

**Maharashtra** approves changes to the '**Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026 (PAHSKY)' (Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Farmer Debt Relief Scheme 2026)**. It removes key eligibility conditions, including the previous \$517.83 (INR 50,000) upper limit applicable to the beneficiaries of the 2019 waiver. It also scraps the condition of regular loan repayment during 2026-27, making nearly 2.3 million farmers eligible for incentive benefits under the scheme.

**Source:** [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/maharashtra-eases-ahilyadevi-holkar-farm-loan-waiver-norms)

**Telangana** releases **Draft Core Urban Region (Integrated Governance) Bill.** The bill is set to replace the decades-old Greater Hyderabad Municipal Corporation (GHMC) Act, 1955 and aims to overhaul urban governance in the Hyderabad Metropolitan Region.

**Source:** [ETV](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/state/telangana-unveils-draft-cure-2026-act-to-replace-ghmc-law-after-seven-decades-enn26070603723)

**In detail:** [The Core Urban Region (Integrated Governance) Bill, 2026](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:ae27a58e-207f-4b4d-8caa-881937be730f)

**Arunachal Pradesh** approves the **Arunachal Human Capital and Economic Transformation Vision 2036**. The vision aims to support long-term development through 2036, focused on two pillars: building human capital (education, skills, health) and driving economic transformation.

**Source:** [North East Today](https://northeasttoday.in/northeast/arunachal-pradesh/arunachal-cabinet-clears-14-landmark-decisions-focus-on-youth-infra-health-and-business-reforms/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://arunachalipr.gov.in/post/cabinet-unveils-sweeping-reform-agenda-approves-rs-7-834-crore-investment-plan)

**Arunachal Pradesh** approves the **Arunachal Pradesh Apiculture and Honey Policy, 2026** The policy aims to establish the state as a leading hub for scientific beekeeping and premium honey production.

**Source:** [North East Today](https://northeasttoday.in/northeast/arunachal-pradesh/arunachal-cabinet-clears-14-landmark-decisions-focus-on-youth-infra-health-and-business-reforms/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://arunachalipr.gov.in/post/cabinet-unveils-sweeping-reform-agenda-approves-rs-7-834-crore-investment-plan)

**Arunachal Pradesh** approves the **Non-Applicability of Trade Licence (for Statutory Licence Holders) Notification, 2026.** Businesses with valid statutory licences will be exempted from obtaining separate Trade Licences for 56 notified activities.

**Source:** [North East Today](https://northeasttoday.in/northeast/arunachal-pradesh/arunachal-cabinet-clears-14-landmark-decisions-focus-on-youth-infra-health-and-business-reforms/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://arunachalipr.gov.in/post/cabinet-unveils-sweeping-reform-agenda-approves-rs-7-834-crore-investment-plan)

**Andhra Pradesh** approves an **Advocates Protection Bill, 2026**. It is the first legislation in the state with the goal to safeguard the lives and property of lawyers practicing in the state.

**Source:** [Arunachal 24](https://arunachal24.in/arunachal-cabinet-clears-advocates-protection-bill-2026/)

**Sikkim** establishes **CM-SAATHI.** The scheme aims to provide free, high-quality coaching and academic support to students preparing for national-level entrance examinations such as JEE and NEET.

**Source:** [India Today](https://www.indiatodayne.in/sikkim/story/sikkim-launches-cm-saathi-to-provide-free-jee-neet-coaching-to-over-2000-students-1425969-2026-07-17)

**West Bengal** announces **mandatory use of Bengali for all government work from September 1** **onwards**. Hindi will be retained for correspondence with the Union government.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/politics-and-nation/west-bengal-to-make-bengali-mandatory-for-all-government-work-from-sept-1/articleshow/132507886.cms)
