**States Weekly Newsletter**

**August 5, 2026**

**Big News: Assam** approves the **Assam Compressed Biogas (CBG) Policy 2026; Meghalaya** approves the **Meghalaya Electric Vehicle (EV) Policy 2026; Gujarat** publishes its **Gujarat Shipbuilding and Repair Policy; Haryana** rolls out a **MSME and Export Promotion Policy; Odisha** clears proposal to introduce the **Odisha Development Authorities (Amendment) Bill, 2026; and more.**

**Health**

**Rajasthan** rolls out a **new digital medical emergency referral system**. Patients referred to tertiary hospitals will have their medical history reach the control room online before they arrive at the hospital, allowing a team of medical experts to review the reports prior to admission.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/rajasthan/digital-medical-emergency-referral-system-set-to-transform-patient-care-in-rajasthan/article71295052.ece/amp/)

**West Bengal** introduces the '**Mukhya Mantri Swasthya Bima Yojana'** (Chief Minister's Health Insurance Scheme). It gives eligible families cashless hospitalization cover of up to \$5,240 (INR 5 lakh) a year, available on a family-floater basis for secondary and tertiary hospital treatment.

**Source:** [Outlook](https://www.outlookmoney.com/personal-finance/west-bengal-rolls-out-rs-5-lakh-health-cover-outside-ayushman-bharat)

**Maharashtra** bans the **sale, distribution, and advertising of junk food in and around 50 meters of schools**. The policy aims to encourage healthier eating habits among children.

**Source:** [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/no-vada-pav-samosa-in-and-outside-maharashtra-schools-food-bodys-diktat-11839943)

**Climate Change and Energy**

**Haryana** approves full exemption of **motor vehicle tax on new Electric Vehicles (EVs)**. The rebate will be applicable to new battery-operated scooters, autos, e-rickshaws, and four-wheelers with an ex-showroom price of up to \$31,428 (INR 30 lakh) registered in the state. On those priced above \$31,428 (INR 30 lakh), a 50 percent rebate has been approved.

**Source:** [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/haryana-approves-100-tax-exemption-on-electric-vehicles-up-to-rs-30-lakh-11835092)

**In detail:** [Press release](https://prharyana.gov.in/en/press-notes-278)

**Assam** approves the **Assam Compressed Biogas (CBG) Policy 2026**. The policy aims to offer a comprehensive framework to convert organic waste into clean energy.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/oil-and-gas/assam-cabinet-clears-cbg-policy-2026-aligned-with-national-model/132768933)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://cm.assam.gov.in/cabinet-decisions-details)

**Meghalaya** approves the **Meghalaya Electric Vehicle (EV) Policy 2026**. Under the new policy, Meghalaya will provide an incentive of \$262 (INR 25,000) to every individual purchasing an electric vehicle, along with a complete waiver on vehicle registration fees.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/guwahati/meghalaya-nod-to-electric-vehicles-policy/articleshow/132693057.cms)

**Chhattisgarh** launches the **Decentralized Women Empowerment on Energy and Policy for Transformative Inclusion (DWEEPTI Yojana)**. It enables rural women's self-help groups to become vendors, technicians, managers, and entrepreneurs to expand the solar power sector.

**Source:** [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/india/2026/Jul/27/chhattisgarh-launches-women-led-solar-policy-under-dweepti-yojana)

**In detail:** [Press release](https://dprcg.gov.in/post/1784890136/Rural-women-in-Chhattisgarh-are-becoming-solar-energy-entrepreneurs-through-the-Deepti-scheme)

**Finance**

Nothing critical to report this week.

**Industrial Policy and Business Regulations**

**Andhra Pradesh** amends the **Andhra Pradesh Building Rules, 2017**. Under the revised rules, buildings measuring 24 meters or more, including stilt floors, are now classified as high-rises. To curb project delays, the government has also introduced a phased fire-clearance system.

**Source:** [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/andhra-pradesh/2026/Jul/30/andhra-govt-notifies-revamped-building-rules-to-boost-ease-of-doing-business)

**Gujarat** publishes its **Gujarat Shipbuilding and Repair Policy**. It envisages setting up two Integrated Mega Shipbuilding Parks, increasing the state's shipbuilding capacity to more than 50 lakh Deadweight Tonnage (DWT) and attracting investments worth over \$2.8 billion (INR 27,000 crore).

**Source:** [Daily Pioneer](https://dailypioneer.com/news/gujarat-unveils-shipbuilding-policy-eyes-more-than-rs-27000-cr-investment)

**In detail:** [Gujarat Shipbuilding and Repair Policy 2026](https://gmbports.org/assets/downloads/Shipbuilding_and_Repair_Policy_new_22052026.pdf)

**Gujarat** enhances the **financial powers of District Collectors and District Development Officers (DDOs) for allotment of government land**. It aims at accelerating infrastructure development, industrial growth, and social and public purpose projects by allowing cases to be resolved at the district level.

**Source:** [Big News Network](https://www.bignewsnetwork.com/news/279214107/gujarat-cm-bhupendra-patel-increases-powers-of-collectors-ddos-for-govt-land-allotments)

**In detail**: [Press release](https://gujaratindia.gov.in/Home/NewsDetailsPage/w7A17oo3qXRuTB%E2%9C%BFMDEhSZw%E2%99%AC%E2%99%AC)

**Haryana** rolls out a **MSME and Export Promotion Policy**. Under a proposed insurance scheme, firms will get up to 33 percent of their insurance premium, and MSME exporters can avail 3 pe cent interest assistance capped at \$10,476 (INR 10 lakh) a year.

**Source:** [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/haryanas-new-msme-policy-eyes-rs-55000-crore-investment-5-lakh-jobs)

**Himachal Pradesh** introduces a **one-time consent certificate from the Himachal Pradesh State Pollution Control Board (HPSPCB).** The certificate aims to ease business operations for industries and has a validity of up to 25 years.

**Source:** [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/himachal-introduces-one-time-consent-certificate-from-pollution-board-for-industries/)

**In detail:** [Press release](http://www.himachalpr.gov.in/OnePressRelease.aspx?Language=1&ID=48405)

**Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations**

**Tamil Nadu** extends deadline to apply for **the formalization of unapproved plots and layouts**. It has been extended to June 30, 2027, granting an additional 12-month extension. It is strictly applicable to unapproved layout plans, and wherein at least one plot was sold and registered, on or before October 20, 2016.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/tamil-nadu/tamil-nadu-government-extends-deadline-for-regularisation-of-unapproved-layouts/article71285539.ece)

**In detail**: [Government Order](https://cms.tn.gov.in/cms_migrated/document/GO/hud_t_148_2026.pdf)

**Assam** passes the **Assam Land and Revenue Regulation (Amendment) Bill 2026.** It strengthens the state's heritage land protection framework by restricting land ownership and purchase within a 5-km radius of "iconic heritage institutions" that are at least 250 years old.

**Source:** [Assam Tribune](https://assamtribune.com/assam/assam-assembly-clears-5-bills-on-budget-sessions-final-day-land-law-sparks-row-1614917)

**In detail:** [The Assam Land and Revenue Regulation (Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.assambidhansabha.org/legislation/bills/introduced/252)

**Assam** approves the **Registration (Assam Amendment) Bill, 2026.** The bill amends rules governing instruments of agreement relating to sale of immovable property, including authorizations for construction, development, or transfer of property

**Source:** [Assam Tribune](https://assamtribune.com/assam/assam-assembly-clears-5-bills-on-budget-sessions-final-day-land-law-sparks-row-1614917)

**In detail:** [The Registration (Assam Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.assambidhansabha.org/legislation/bills/introduced/254)

**Assam** clears **Indian Stamp (Assam Amendment) Bill, 2026.** It extends the 2024 belt framework, tightens the definition of "original inhabitant" and gives District Commissioners new power to evict those who fail to quality.

**Source:** [Assam Tribune](https://assamtribune.com/assam/assam-assembly-clears-5-bills-on-budget-sessions-final-day-land-law-sparks-row-1614917)

**In detail:** [The Indian Stamp (Assam Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.assambidhansabha.org/legislation/bills/introduced/255)

**Assam** passes the **Assam Fixation of Ceiling on Land Holdings (Amendment) Bill, 2026.** It is designed to modernize and expand the use of tea garden land, addressing both economic and social needs.

**Source:** [Assam Tribune](https://assamtribune.com/assam/assam-assembly-clears-5-bills-on-budget-sessions-final-day-land-law-sparks-row-1614917)

**In detail:** [The Assam Fixation of Ceiling on Land Holdings (Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://www.assambidhansabha.org/legislation/bills/introduced/253)

**Ladakh** authorizes **Divisional Commissioners under the land-use regulations framework to approve change in land use**. It is applicable to land measuring above 2 kanals and up to 4 kanals. A "kanal" is roughly 0.125 acres**.** The Chief Secretary will approve applications for land measuring above 4 kanals and up to 2 acres.

**Source:** [Daily Excelsior](https://www.dailyexcelsior.com/lg-decentralizes-land-use-approval-powers-in-ladakh-to-speed-up-development/)

**In detail:** [Press release](https://ladakh.gov.in/l-g-vk-saxena-extends-pro-people-land-use-deregulation-in-ladakh-authorizes-chief-secretary-and-divisional-commissioner-to-approve-change-in-land-use-on-land-up-to-2-acres/)

**Infrastructure and Governance**

**Bihar** approves **Anganwadi Sevika and Sahayika Selection (Amendment) Guidelines, 2026**. It paves the way for the recruitment of more than 22,000 'Anganwadi' (Rural Childcare Centers) 'Sevikas' (Workers) and 'Sahayikas' (Helpers) under the Integrated Child Development Services (ICDS).

**Source:** [APAC news](https://apacnewsnetwork.com/2026/07/bihar-cabinet-clears-major-welfare-infrastructure-push-22000-anganwadi-recruitments-approved/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/1/Smart%20City/Cabinet%20Decisions/Cabinet%20Press%2029.07.2026.pdf)

**Bihar** approves the **Bihar Sports Policy 2026**. It aims to promote sports culture and identify talent across the state.

**Source:** [APAC news](https://apacnewsnetwork.com/2026/07/bihar-cabinet-clears-major-welfare-infrastructure-push-22000-anganwadi-recruitments-approved/)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://state.bihar.gov.in/cache/1/Smart%20City/Cabinet%20Decisions/Cabinet%20Press%2029.07.2026.pdf)

**Delhi** approves '**Delhi Lakshmi Yojana' (Delhi Goddess of Wealth Scheme).** Eligible women will receive \$26.2 (INR 2,500) per month through direct benefit transfer.

**Source:** [India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/cities/delhi/story/delhi-lakshmi-yojana-approved-rs-2500-monthly-aid-for-women-portal-opens-august-1-2958381-2026-07-28)

**Haryana** approves a **new formula-based Property Tax Regime**. It replaces the 2013 property tax framework with a uniform, capital value-based assessment system.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/haryana-clears-new-property-tax-formula-housing-policy/articleshow/132691861.cms)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions - Press release](https://prharyana.gov.in/en/press-notes-278)

**Haryana** approves an **exemption and rebate framework.** Under this policy, religious institutions, government schools and hospitals, agricultural properties, 'gaushalas' (cow sanctuaries), orphanages, cremation grounds, and several charitable institutions will be exempt from property tax.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/haryana-clears-new-property-tax-formula-housing-policy/articleshow/132691861.cms)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions - Press release](https://prharyana.gov.in/en/press-notes-278)

**Assam** approves the **Assam Legal Metrology (Government Approved Test Center) Rules, 2026**. It implements Self-Certification by Manufacturers and Third-Party Certification of Weighing and Measuring Instruments as part of the government's compliance reduction and deregulation reforms.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/oil-and-gas/assam-cabinet-clears-cbg-policy-2026-aligned-with-national-model/132768933)

**In detail:** [Cabinet decisions](https://cm.assam.gov.in/cabinet-decisions-details)

**Mizoram** approves guidelines for the **Inner Line Pass (ILP).** It aims at strengthening the regulation and monitoring of entry of non-residents into the state. The approval of the revised ILP framework is expected to streamline procedures related to issuance, monitoring, and enforcement of Inner Line passes in Mizoram.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://travel.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/destination/states/mizoram-cabinet-approves-enhanced-ilp-guidelines-to-regulate-non-resident-entry/131199379)

**Odisha** clears proposal to introduce the **Odisha Development Authorities (Amendment) Bill, 2026**. It will amend the existing Odisha Development Authorities Act, 1982 in favor of constituting "Economic Region Development Authorities."

**Source:** [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/india-news/odisha-cabinet-clears-bill-for-economic-region-authorities-101785658175537.html)

**Any Other**

**Gujarat** launches a **Marketing Support Scheme.** Rural women associated the Gujarat Livelihood Promotion Company Limited (GLPC) will get \$2.6 million (INR 25 crore) during 2026-27 to connect their products to e-commerce platforms, giving them access to national and international markets.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/amp/news/policy/gujarat-launches-25-crore-marketing-support-scheme-to-connect-sakhi-mandal-products-to-e-commerce-platforms/132763915)
