**States Weekly Newsletter**

**September 9, 2026**

**Big News: Uttar Pradesh** issues a draft **Uttar Pradesh Data Center Policy 2026; Assam** approves an amendment to the **Industrial and Investment Policy of Assam, 2019; Manipur** approves the draft **Manipur Unified Trade, Shops and Establishment Licensing Bill, 2026; Haryana** passes the **Haryana Right to Business Bill, 2026; Maharashtra** launches a **Maharashtra Land Bank Digital System; Nagaland** passes the **Frontier Nagaland Territorial Authority (FNTA) Bill 2026;** and more.

**Health**

**Maharashtra** announces a **drug eradication campaign**. The state aims to eradicate drug abuse by 2029 through extensive public awareness.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://health.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/policy/maharashtra-government-approves-policy-to-eradicate-drugs-by-2029-awareness-strict-action-planned/133708024)

**Climate Change and Energy**

**Delhi** revises its **solar policy.** Under the policy, households with monthly electricity consumption of 400 units or above will be eligible for free installation of 3 KW rooftop solar panels.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/renewable/delhi-cabinet-approves-revised-solar-policy-targets-free-3-kw-panels-for-2-30-lakh-consumers/133696108)

**Telangana** fixes the **Average Pooled Power Purchase Cost (APPC) for DISCOMs (Distribution Companies) at \$0.056 (INR 5.330) per kWh for the Financial Year 2025-26.** The order was issued by the Telangana Electricity Regulatory Commission (TGERC).

Source: [Solar Quarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/09/03/tgerc-fixes-telangana-appc-at-inr-5-330-kwh-for-2025-26/)

In detail: [TGERC order](https://www.tgerc.telangana.gov.in/file_upload/uploads/Orders/Commission%20Orders/2026/Final%20Pooled%20Power%20Order.pdf)

**Goa** launches the **Goa 'Ujjwala Yojana' (Luminous Scheme), 2026**. Under the scheme, eligible households will receive annual financial assistance of \$31.76 (INR 3,000) towards cooking-fuel expenses for the Financial Year 2026-27. The assistance, equivalent to the cost of three domestic LPG refills, will be paid as a one-time lump sum directly into the beneficiary's Aadhaar-linked bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT).

**Source:** [The Goan](https://www.thegoan.net/goa-news/cm-launches-goa-ujjwala-yojana-announces-rs-3000-annual-lpg-assistance/153759.html)

**Finance**

Nothing critical to report this week.

**Industrial Policy and Business Regulations**

**Gujarat** approves the **Gujarat Industrial Development Corporation (GIDC) Amnesty Scheme.** The scheme aims to resolve pending stamp-duty issues linked to past transfers of industrial plots, which were approved via allotment letters or supplementary agreements instead of registered deeds.**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/gujarat-amnesty-plan-msmes-to-get-up-to-80-stamp-duty-relief-on-past-gidc-plot-transfers/articleshow/133690190.cms)

**In detail:** [Government Order](https://revenuedepartment.gujarat.gov.in/downloads/noti_01092026.pdf)

**Uttar Pradesh** issues a draft **Uttar Pradesh Data Center Policy 2026**. The policy offers exemptions on transmission and wheeling charges for Data Centers that meet specific renewable energy consumption targets.

**Source:** [MVA pulse](https://www.mvapulse.com/uttar-pradesh-unveils-renewable-energy-incentives-for-data-centers/)

**In detail:** [Draft Uttar Pradesh Data Center Policy 2026](https://invest.up.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/UPDCP-2026-Notification-English.pdf)

**Goa** passes the **Goa Registration of Tourist Trade (Amendment) Bill, 2026**. The bill makes it mandatory for shacks operating on private land within the Coastal Regulation Zone (CRZ) to register with Goa's Tourism Department.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/india/goa-tightens-rules-private-shacks-in-crz-now-need-to-register-with-tourism-department/articleshow/133724389.cms)

**In detail:** [Goa Registration of Tourist Trade (Amendment) Bill, 2026](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:c997a595-8f25-4334-ae19-f0a472504dae)

**Assam** approves an amendment to the **Industrial and Investment Policy of Assam, 2019.** The revision includes Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities (RVSFs) as an eligible "industries activity" for fiscal incentives as provided under the Vehicle Scrappage Policy of Assam, 2022.

**Source**: [Guwahati Plus](https://www.guwahatiplus.com/assam/assam-cabinet-approves-land-in-nagaon-kamrup-for-industrial-estates-approves-new-business-reforms)

**Manipur** approves the draft **Manipur Unified Trade, Shops and Establishment Licensing Bill, 2026.** The Bill proposes a single unified licensing system for trade, shops and establishments across the state. It aims to eliminate overlapping licenses, reduce regulatory burdens, and simplify procedures for businesses.

**Source:** [North East Today](https://northeasttoday.in/northeast/manipur-cabinet-approves-unified-trade-licensing-bill/)

**Goa** passes the **Goa Ease of Doing Business (Miscellaneous Amendments) Bill, 2026.** The bill amends five state laws relating to court fees, industrial development, time-bound delivery of public services, shops and establishments, and town and country planning.

**Source:** [DD News](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/09/ease-of-doing-business-goa-assembly-passes-bill-revising-stamp-vendor-rules-womens-work-timings/)

**In detail:** [Goa Ease of Doing Business (Miscellaneous Amendments) Bill, 2026](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:6c1384c9-1194-4b91-b898-b6fc0f33263d)

**Assam** passes the **Assam Trade Articles (Licensing and Control) (Amendment) Order, 2026.** The order abolishes the requirement for a Retail Selling License for petroleum products.

**Source:** [Guwahati Plus](https://www.guwahatiplus.com/assam/assam-cabinet-approves-land-in-nagaon-kamrup-for-industrial-estates-approves-new-business-reforms)

**Haryana** passes the **Haryana Right to Business Bill, 2026.** The bill introduces a "Right to Business" framework allowing eligible enterprises to commence operations through time-bound in-principal approvals and a 36-month moratorium on inspections, reducing procedural barriers and promoting ease of doing business.

**Source:** [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/11-key-bills-cleared-as-haryana-vidhan-sabha-concludes-monsoon-session/)

**In detail:** [Haryana Right to Business Bill, 2026](https://www.egazetteharyana.gov.in/Gazette/Extra-Ordinary/2026/133-2026-Ext/32945.pdf)

**Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations**

**Andhra Pradesh** introduces a **Generic Pharmacy Scheme**. The scheme, aimed at youth from the Backward Classes (BC) community, aims to create employment opportunities by encouraging eligible pharmacy graduates to open generic pharmaceutical shops. The state will provide \$4,808 (INR 4 lakh) as a subsidy.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/education/news/andhra-pradesh-launches-generic-pharmacy-scheme-for-bc-youth-offers-4-lakh-subsidy-for-self-employment/articleshow/133735388.cms)

**Andhra Pradesh** reclassifies **22.31 acres of land in Nellore Urban District**. The land is reclassified from "Canal Poramboke" (land reserved along canals for maintenance, irrigation access, and water flow) to "Assessed Waste-Dry" (unproductive waste land). The land will be distributed to poor beneficiaries in the area for housing.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/Vijayawada/andhra-pradesh-cabinet-clears-reclassification-of-2231-acres-in-nellore-for-house-sites/article71426538.ece)

**Maharashtra** launches a **Maharashtra Land Bank Digital System**. The system aims to bring all government-owned land onto a single platform to better manage allotment and speedier approvals for infrastructure development.

**Source:** [Swarajya](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/maharashtra-launches-integrated-digital-land-bank-to-speed-infrastructure-development-and-identify-land-for-afforestation)

**Uttar Pradesh** launches the **Uttar Pradesh Corporation for Outsource Services (UPCOS).** UPCOS aims to streamline recruitment, guarantee fixed salaries, and safeguard the statutory rights of contractual workers.

**Source:** [Hindu Business line](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/uttar-pradesh-launches-upcos-to-safeguard-rights-and-salaries-of-outsourced-workers/article71419809.ece)

**In detail:** [Government Order](https://upcos.org/outer-assets-new/images/GovernmentOrder.pdf) (in Hindi); [UPCOS](https://upcos.org/) (Portal)

**Ladakh** approves the **Ladakh Autonomous Hill Development Councils (Nautor Regularization) Rules, 2026**. Nautor Lands are government-granted lands historically assigned to village service providers in lieu of payment for their hereditary duties. The new rules provide for proprietary rights over up to 10 acres. Holdings beyond 10 acres may be allocated on a leasehold basis.

**Source:** [ETV](https://www.etvbharat.com/en/bharat/big-land-reform-in-ladakh-lg-saxena-approves-rules-for-regularisation-of-60000-acres-of-nautor-land-enn26090406285); [Press Release](https://ladakh.gov.in/l-g-vk-saxena-approves-new-lahdc-nautor-regularisation-rules-2026-paving-way-for-proprietary-rights-of-nautor-land-authority-for-allotting-nautor-land-now-vested-in-lahdcs/)

**Haryana** passes the **Haryana Village Common Lands (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026.** The bill delegates the power to approve the sale of 'Shamlat' (village common) land to Deputy Commissioners, decentralizing the process to expedite pending applications from residents who constructed homes on such land before March 31, 2004.

**Source:** [Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/haryana/11-key-bills-cleared-as-haryana-vidhan-sabha-concludes-monsoon-session/)

**In detail:** [Haryana Village Common Lands (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026](https://www.egazetteharyana.gov.in/Gazette/Extra-Ordinary/2026/130-2026-Ext/32923.pdf)

**Goa** passes the **Goa Children's (Amendment) Bill, 2026.** The bill seeks to remove references to "leprosy" with the aim to eliminate social stigma and discrimination against people affected by the disease.

**Himachal Pradesh** passes the **Himachal Pradesh Panchayati Raj (Second Amendment) Bill, 2026.** The bill expands the definition of a land encroacher's family to include daughters-in-law, disqualifying any candidate whose family member, including a daughter-in-law, is found guilty of encroaching on government, panchayat, municipal, or cooperative society land from contesting panchayat elections for six years.
