**States Weekly Newsletter**

**August 12, 2026**

**Big News: Delhi** suspends the **Central Procurement Agency's (CPA) procurement functions as an interim measure; Tamil Nadu** announces a **Carbon Trading Scheme; Delhi** approves a draft **Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026; Chhattisgarh** approves the **Chhattisgarh State Artificial Intelligence (AI) Mission; Andhra Pradesh** approves a draft **Public Private Partnership (PPP Policy) 2026; Tripura** announces an **Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Policy under the Jal Jeevan Mission.**

**Health**

**Andhra Pradesh** releases draft **regulations for** **private hospitals, diagnostic centers, and clinics offering AYUSH (ayurveda, yoga, and naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and homeopathy) services.** As per the new regulations, registrations will be made mandatory for every private AYUSH facility.

**Source:** [Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/andhra-pradesh/ap-govt-issues-draft-regulations-for-private-ayush-facilities/article71310246.ece)

**Delhi** suspends the **Central Procurement Agency's (CPA) procurement functions as an interim measure.** Delhi has directed state-run hospitals to obtain medicines and medical equipment on their own until a new centralized procurement framework is decided. The CPA, established in 1994, is an agency which procures medicines and surgical equipment for all Delhi government-run hospitals.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://government.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/healthcare/delhi-suspends-central-procurement-system-hospitals-to-buy-medical-supplies-directly/133047652)

**Gujarat** launches a "**Scheme to Provide Assistance for Organ Transplants of Kidney, Liver, Heart, Pancreas, etc., and for Operations Including Robotic Surgeries for Patients Belonging to the Scheduled Tribe Community of the State**." The new welfare scheme for Scheduled Tribe (ST) patients suffering from serious illnesses comes with an allocation of \$0.2 million (INR 2 crore) for the Financial Year 2026-27.

**Source:** [Devdiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/headlines/3959361-gujarat-govt-announces-rs-2-cr-welfare-scheme-for-tribal-patients-needing-organ-transplants-robotic-surgeries)

**Climate Change and Energy**

**Haryana** amends **HERC (Electricity Supply Code) Regulations, 2014.** The regulations, issued by the Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC), relax surcharge norms for excess contract demand. Consumers will no longer have to pay a surcharge if their power draw remains within 110 percent of the approved contract demand. Earlier, any draw beyond 105 percent attracted a 25 percent surcharge on total electricity charges.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/chandigarh/herc-eases-power-surcharge-norms-offers-relief-to-consumers/articleshow/132871602.cms)

**In detail:** [Notification](https://herc.org.in/WriteReadData/Pdf/R20260722.pdf)

**Odisha** releases draft **OERC (Grid Interactive Distributed Renewable Energy Sources) Regulations, 2026.** The regulations, issued by the Odisha Electricity Regulatory Commission (OERC) aim at establishing a comprehensive regulatory framework for the integration of Distributed Renewable Energy Systems (DRES).

**Source:** [Energetica](https://energetica-india.net/news/oerc-releases-draft-grid-interactive-distributed-renewable-energy-regulations-2026-for-odisha)

**In detail:** [Draft OERC (Grid Interactive Distributed Renewable Energy Sources) Regulations, 2026](https://www.orierc.org/UploadData/LatestUpdates/7945c1ba-7c35-45bd-a4fb-d647c3ff80d7.pdf)

**Tamil Nadu** announces a **Carbon Trading Scheme.** Farmers adopting sustainable cultivation practices such as Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) and Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD) irrigation would be able to earn additional income through carbon credits.

**Source:** [Mathrubhumi](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/news/india/tamil-nadu-agriculture-budget-2026-stalin-reaction-climate-carbon-trading-lmnk66yv)

**In detail:** [Press Release.](https://newsonair.gov.in/tamil-nadu-government-presents-revised-agriculture-budget-for-2026-27-in-the-state-assembly/) [Budget Highlights 2026-27](https://financedept.tn.gov.in/en/my-documents/2020/07/RBE-2026-27_Highlights_English_Final.pdf)

**Tamil Nadu** announces a **\$63 million (INR 600 crore) Soil Fertility Mission**. The Mission aims to restore the soils organic carbon, reclaim saline and alkaline soils, and reduce dependence on chemical fertilizers.

**Source:** [Mathrubhumi](https://english.mathrubhumi.com/news/india/tamil-nadu-agriculture-budget-2026-stalin-reaction-climate-carbon-trading-lmnk66yv)

**In detail:** [Press Release.](https://newsonair.gov.in/tamil-nadu-government-presents-revised-agriculture-budget-for-2026-27-in-the-state-assembly/) [Budget Highlights 2026-27](https://financedept.tn.gov.in/en/my-documents/2020/07/RBE-2026-27_Highlights_English_Final.pdf)

**Finance**

Nothing critical to report this week.

**Industrial Policy and Business Regulations**

**Delhi** approves a draft **Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026.** The bill aims at making it easier for businesses to start and operate. The law introduces deemed approvals, a self-certification system for low-risk businesses, and a single-window clearance system to reduce delays.

**Source:** [Silicon India](https://www.siliconindia.com/news/general/delhi-cabinet-clears-business-reform-bill-with-singlewindow-deemed-approvals-nid-241816-cid-1.html)

**Haryana** amends the process of **setting up gas stations**. The service of issuing "No Objection Certificates (NOCs)" for new gas stations has now been included within the scope of the Haryana "Right to Service." Under the new policy, NOCs must be issued within 45 days.

**Source:** [Dhakad Haryana](https://dhakadharyana.com/haryana-news-petrol-pump-noc-will-now-be-issued-in-45-days-government-has-fixed-the-time-limit/)

**In detail:** [Press Release](https://prharyana.gov.in/en/press-notes-286)

**Andhra Pradesh** issues an integrated **Pedestrian Safety and Universal Accessibility Policy, 2026.** The policy seeks to shift road planning from a vehicle-centric approach to people-centric public spaces, with the safety and accessibility of pedestrians as its primary goal.

**Source:** [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/ap-notifies-nations-first-integrated-pedestrian-safety-policy-in-india-1977471)

**In detail:** [Pedestrian Safety and Universal Accessibility Policy, 2026](https://apmtptoa.blogspot.com/2026/08/gomsno-166-dated-07-08-2026-andhra.html?m=0)

**Jammu and Kashmir** announces their **Legal Metrology (Enforcement) Rules, 2026.** The rules introduce several measures: licenses for manufacturer, dealers, and repairers of weights and measures have been replaced with registration certificates. The earlier requirement for the renewal of these certificates has been abolished.

**Source:** [Greater Kashmir](https://www.greaterkashmir.com/jammu-kashmir/govt-notifies-legal-metrology-enforcement-rules-to-promote-eodb-consumer-protection-12240473)

**In detail:** [Jammu & Kashmir Legal Metrology (Enforcement) Rules, 2026](https://law.jk.gov.in/download.php?id=6061)

**Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations**

**Goa** announces policy to **allow citizens to register property-related documents at any Sub-Registrar Office**. The "Anywhere Registration within the District" policy aims to enhance access and reduce wait times for property registration.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://realty.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/residential/goa-government-allows-citizens-to-register-property-document-at-any-sub-registrar-office-in-district/133045759)

**In detail:** [Notification](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:246181ba-143f-4787-8b19-b93a6397df75)

**Infrastructure and Governance**

**Andhra Pradesh** approves a draft **Public Private Partnership (PPP Policy) 2026.** The policy aims at attracting private investment in sectors such as transport, ports, airports, healthcare, education and renewable energy. The policy provides for \$180 million (INR 1,500 crore) under the Viability Gap Funding (VGF) Scheme and a "Bridge Fund" to support projects with long-term revenue potential.

**Source:** [The Print](https://theprint.in/india/andhra-cabinet-approves-ppp-policy-withdraws-disha-bill-clears-key-amaravati-projects/3007677/)

**In detail:** [Public Private Partnership (PPP Policy) 2026](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:c48465f6-8ace-489d-a5b3-c36e49e92376)

**Gujarat** announces **financial assistance of \$21 (INR 2,000) for students who successfully complete a 20-day internship in Semester 6 of their Undergraduate Programs**. The stipend applies to those enrolled in government and grant-in-aid arts, commerce, and science colleges affiliated with state-run universities.

**Source:** [Times of India](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/city/ahmedabad/nep-gujarat-government-boosts-mandatory-college-internships-with-rs-2000-aid/amp_articleshow/133014593.cms)

**Maharashtra** increases the subsidy under the **Pink E-Auto Scheme from 20 percent to 40 percent.** The Pink E-Auto Scheme aims at creating livelihood opportunities for women and promoting clean mobility. Women who have already received their Pink E-Rickshaws will also be eligible for the additional 20 percent subsidy to help repay loans taken to purchase their vehicles.

**Source:** [Indian Express](https://indianexpress.com/article/cities/pune/maharashtra-pink-e-auto-scheme-40-percent-subsidy-experts-concerns-10817848/)

**In detail:** [Order](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:c949f296-77dc-4203-bf42-aed1e62a5d9b)

**Chhattisgarh** approves the **Chhattisgarh State Artificial Intelligence (AI) Mission.** The Mission is intended to promote emerging technologies and skill development. It aims to establish 100 AI Data Labs and five Centers of Excellence in the next five years.

**Source:** [The Statesman](https://www.thestatesman.com/india/chhattisgarh-cabinet-approves-%E2%82%B9500-crore-ai-mission-beml-plant-and-key-governance-reforms-2-1503624803.html)

**In detail:** [Cabinet Decision](https://dprcg.gov.in/pages/1768982672/%E0%A4%AE%E0%A4%82%E0%A4%A4%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%AA%E0%A4%B0%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B7%E0%A4%A6-%E0%A4%95%E0%A5%87-%E0%A4%A8%E0%A4%BF%E0%A4%B0%E0%A5%8D%E0%A4%A3%E0%A4%AF-2026)

**Punjab** approves the **Punjab Regulation of Fee of Unaided Educational Institutions (Amendment) Bill, 2026.** The bill seeks to regulate unnecessary and unreasonable fee increases by private unaided educational institutions.

**Source:** [Hindustan Times](https://www.hindustantimes.com/cities/chandigarh-news/punjab-cabinet-clears-fee-regulation-bill-approves-3-digital-open-varsities-101785954112899.html#google_vignette)

**In detail:** [Punjab Regulation of Fee of Un-aided Educational Institutions (Amendment) Ordinance, 2026](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_states/punjab/2026/Ord2of2026PB.pdf)

**West Bengal** announces a **38 percent increase in Dearness Allowance (DA) and Dearness Relief (DR) for state government employees and pensioners, with effect from October 1, 2026.**

**Source:** [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/money/personal-finance/da-hike-dr-west-bengal-govt-notifies-38-pc-dearness-allowance-wages-increase-employees-pensioners-daily-worker-1-october-11786094501968.html)

**In detail:** [Press Release](https://ddindia.co.in/2026/08/west-bengal-govt-notifies-enhanced-da-payment-from-oct-1/)

**Water and Sanitation**

**Tripura** announces an **Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Policy under the Jal Jeevan Mission.** The initiative aims to make village-level water supply systems more reliable, sustainable, and community-driven, ensuring that problems are quickly resolved without waiting for higher-level intervention.

**Source:** [Tripura Times](https://tripuratimes.com/ttimes/tripura-introduces-first-operation-maintenance-policy-to-strengthen-rural-drinking-water-supply-40153.html/)

**In detail:** [Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Policy](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:7f6bb332-cdb7-4fbe-ab74-481c46c6ce5d)

**Center - State Relations**

**The Union government** notifies the **Readjustment of Representation of Scheduled Tribes (ST) in Assembly Constituencies of the State of Goa Act, 2025.** The Act grants political reservation for the Scheduled Tribe (ST) community in the 40-member Goa Legislative Assembly. 4 seats in Goa's Assembly will now be reserved for ST candidates.

**Source:** [The Print](https://theprint.in/india/centre-notifies-act-paves-way-for-political-reservation-for-tribals-in-goa-assembly/3007819/)

**In detail:** [Readjustment of Representation of Scheduled Tribes (ST) in Assembly Constituencies of the State of Goa Act, 2025](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/bills_parliament/2024/Readjustment_of_Representation-ST-in-Assembly_Constituencies-Goa_Bill_2024.pdf)
