States Weekly Newsletter
September 16, 2026

Big News: Gujarat passes the Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026; Karnataka passes the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments (Amendment) Bill, 2026; Karnataka approves the Karnataka Aerospace Policy 2026-2031; Tamil Nadu passes the Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning (Amendment) Bill, 2026; Arunachal Pradesh passes the Arunachal Pradesh Airport Area Development Authority Bill, 2026; Chhattisgarh approves the Chhattisgarh Export Promotion Policy 2026-30; and more.

Health 

Chandigarh publishes draft Health Service Rules, 2026. The rules re-define how government doctors advance in their careers and introduce service conditions, including mandatory military service requirements and strict limits on outside hiring. 
Source: Times of India
In detail: The UT Chandigarh Health Service Rules, 2026

Climate Change and Energy 

Andhra Pradesh amends their General Terms and Conditions of Supply (GTCS) for electricity. The amendments introduced by the Andhra Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (APERC) focus on reducing criminal action for minor electricity-related offenses and replacing them with clearer financial penalties. 
Source: Solar Quarter

Maharashtra approves a transmission tariff for renewable energy (RE) projects. The tariff, approved by the Maharashtra Electricity Regulatory Commission (MERC) is essential for facilitating the integration of large-scale renewable energy capacity into the state grid. 
Source: MVA Pulse

Andhra Pradesh introduces an energy roadmap to guarantee uninterrupted 24x7 quality power supply with a view also to mitigate the impact of El Niño conditions.  The initiative is designed to eliminate peak-demand deficits, reduce procurement costs, and enhance grid stability. Under Section 63 of the Electricity Act, 2003, Andhra Pradesh will procure 1,600mw of thermal base-load power through tariff-based competitive bidding on a Design, Build, Finance, Own, and Operate (DBFOO) Framework for the next 12–25 years.
Source: Deccan Chronicle

Finance

Nothing critical to report this week.

Industrial Policy and Business Regulations  

Gujarat passes the Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026. The bill aims to simplify regulations for industries. It enables 24x7 commercial operations, self-declaration for new businesses, and risk-based fire safety. 
Source: Gujarat Samachar
In detail: Gujarat Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026

Gujarat passes the Gujarat Goods and Services Tax (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The amendments aim to ensure consistency between the Central GST Framework and Gujarat’s Goods and Services Tax Act of 2017. 
Source: Times of India

Gujarat passes the Gujarat Live-stock Feed, Poultry Feed and Mineral Mixture Bill, 2026. The bill aims to regulate the manufacture, storage, distribution, sale and quality control of livestock and poultry feed.
Source: Times of India
In detail:  Gujarat Live-stock Feed, Poultry Feed and Mineral Mixture Bill, 2026 (PDF)

Haryana introduces the Haryana EV for Cargo Logistic Scheme. The scheme offers direct financial reimbursement to Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) acquiring commercial electric vehicles. 
Source: Economic Times
In detail: Haryana EV for Cargo Logistic Scheme

Karnataka passes the Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill deletes a set of provisions that prescribes specific transport, monitoring, and security safeguards for women employees working night shifts.
Source: Deccan Chronicle
In detail: Karnataka Shops and Commercial Establishments (Amendment) Act, 2026

Karnataka approves the Karnataka Aerospace Policy 2026-2031. The policy focuses on aerospace, defense, aviation and space research and development and estimates about $6.28 billion (INR 60,000 crore) in investment over the next five years. 
Source: The Hindu

Goa extends the Beach Shack Policy (2023-26) by one year. Shack licenses for the 2026-27 tourism season will be issued by the Tourism Department from September 10, 2026, onwards under the existing fee structure, with no increase. 
Source: Navhind times
In detail: Goa State Shack Policy (2023-2026)

Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations  

Gujarat passes the Gujarat Rent Bill, 2026. The bill offers a new legal framework to regulate rental premises and aims to protect the rights of both landlords and tenants.
Source: Hindustan Times
In detail: Gujarat Rent Bill, 2026

Gujarat passes the Gujarat Land Revenue (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill aims to expedite the certification of genuine, registered land sale documents. 
Source: Desh Gujarat
In detail: Gujarat Land Revenue (Amendment) Bill, 2026

Tamil Nadu passes the Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill updates the rules governing land use, development approvals, and spatial planning across towns and cities in the state.
Source: The Hindu
In detail: Tamil Nadu Town and Country Planning (Amendment) Bill, 2026

Arunachal Pradesh passes the Arunachal Pradesh Airport Area Development Authority Bill, 2026. The bill provides the framework for an Airport Area Development Authority to ensure planned, regulated and sustainable development in and around airport areas.
Source: NorthEast Today

Infrastructure and Governance

Delhi expands the scope of the Mukhyamantri Khel Protsahan Yojana (Chief Minister's Sports Promotion Scheme; MKPY). The scheme now includes financial assistance of $20.9 (INR 2,000) per month for eligible athletes undergoing regular training at Delhi government-run sports centers. 
Source: ETV

Gujarat passes the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill brings government-aided private secondary and higher secondary schools within the scope of the Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Act, 1972. 
Source: Indian Express
In detail: Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (Amendment) Bill, 2026

Maharashtra revises their Sports Policy. The new policy reserves five percent of seats in government jobs for sportspersons. 
Source: New Indian Express

Tamil Nadu passes the Tamil Nadu Private Universities (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill mandates not less than 100 acres of contiguous land will be required for the establishment of a private university. The bill also mandates the sponsoring body to establish a permanent endowment fund of at least $5.23 million (INR50 crore). 
Source: The Hindu

Telangana passes the Telanagna Praja Viswasam (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026. The bill aims at improving ease of doing business and decriminalizes 180 minor offenses. 
Source: Deccan Chronicle

Arunachal Pradesh passes the Arunachal Pradesh Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (Facilitation, Establishment and Operation) Bill, 2026. The bill establishes a framework to streamline the registration, setup, and operations of MSMEs.
Source: NorthEast Today

Arunachal Pradesh passes the Arunachal Pradesh Fire and Emergency Services (Amendment) Bill, 2026. The bill aims to strengthen the existing legal framework for fire prevention, fire safety compliance, and emergency response across the state
Source: NorthEast Today

Chhattisgarh approves the Chhattisgarh Export Promotion Policy 2026-30. The policy focuses on taking value-added agricultural and industrial products to international markets. 
Source: Daily Pioneer
In detail: Cabinet Decisions

Goa announces a new Goa Victim Compensation Scheme, 2026. The scheme offers the victims of rape, human trafficking and other crimes causing severe mental agony to women and children up to $11,900 (INR 10 lakh) in compensation. 
Source: The Goan
In detail: Goa Victim Compensation Scheme, 2026

Meghalaya launches the Service Conduct Rules Book. The rules aim at strengthening professionalism, discipline, and accountability among officials and employees of the Garo Hills Autonomous District Council (GHADC).
Source: India Today
In detail: Press Release

Odisha approves draft Odisha Jan Vishwas (Amendment of Provisions) Bill, 2026. The bill, originally drafted by the Industries Department, proposes amendments to 62 provisions across 16 Acts administered by 11 departments.
Source: New Indian Express

Water and Sanitation 

Haryana announces financial relief to rural sanitation workers The relief includes honorarium arrears, higher uniform and washing allowances, an out-of-village duty allowance of $2.40 (INR 200) per day, compassionate employment for families of workers who die on duty, and a penalty for delayed salary disbursement. 
Source: Hindustan Times
