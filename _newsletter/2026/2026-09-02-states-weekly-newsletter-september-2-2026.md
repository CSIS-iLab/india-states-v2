**States Weekly Newsletter**

**September 2, 2026**

**Big News: Uttarakhand** approves the **Uttarakhand Motor Vehicles Taxation Reform Act; Maharashtra** issues rules for the **Maharashtra Global Capability Center (GCC) Policy, 2025; Uttar Pradesh** launches the **Uttar Pradesh Startup Policy, 2026; Puducherry** passes the **Puducherry Business Reforms Bill, 2026; Uttar Pradesh** approves the **Uttar Pradesh Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2026;** and more.

**Health**

**Telangana** releases guidelines for the **New Employees Health Scheme (NEHS).** The guidelines raise the cashless medical coverage limit from \$2,093 to \$3,140 (INR 2 lakh to 3 lakh) per episode of illness, with no limit on the total number of episodes.

**Source:** [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/telangana/telangana-health-scheme-coverage-raised-to-3-lakh-1981631)

**In detail:** [Order](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:a1deb22e-87cf-47af-b3f5-dcba4ef5164a)

**Climate Change and Energy**

**Gujarat** amends **GERC (Terms and Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Sixth Amendment) Regulations, 2026.** The regulations issued by the Gujarat Electricity Regulatory Commission (GERC) reduce banking charges for green energy open access consumers to \$0.010 from 0.015 (INR 1.5 to 1) per unit.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/renewable/gujarat-cuts-green-power-banking-charge-by-33/133557252)

**In detail:** [GERC (Terms & Conditions for Green Energy Open Access) (Sixth Amendment) Regulations, 2026](https://www.gercin.org/viewdocument/TmV3TmV3c18xMDdfMTc4NzMwMzkzOA==)

**Karnataka** increases **per unit charges to consumers generating solar rooftop power and selling it to the grid**. The Karnataka Electricity Regulatory Commission (KERC) directed that consumers selling solar rooftop power to the grid will get \$0.0407 (INR 3.89) per unit, without subsidy, up from \$0.0404 (INR3.86) per unit earlier.

**Source:** [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/karnataka/2026/Aug/27/karnataka-electricity-regulatory-commission-fixes-rate-for-solar-power-sale-to-grid)

**In detail:** [KERC Order](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:16f23812-b7d0-4035-bb93-7eb4f75bf6dc)

**Assam** proposes the **AERC (Electricity Ombudsman) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026**. The amendment seeks to modify the Assam Electricity Regulatory Commission (AERC) (Electricity Ombudsman) Regulations, 2022, particularly the provisions governing the pay, allowances, and other benefits of the Electricity Ombudsman.

**Source:** [Solar Quarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/08/25/aerc-proposes-second-amendment-to-electricity-ombudsman-regulations-2026-in-assam/)

**In detail:** [Assam Electricity Regulatory Commission (Electricity Ombudsman) (Second Amendment), Regulations, 2026](https://aerc.gov.in/notice-files/1787303403.pdf)

**Himachal Pradesh** approves **HPERC Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive System (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2015**. The amendment, issued by the Himachal Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (HPERC), aims to simplify the process for consumers installing rooftop solar systems in the state.

**Source:** [Solar Quarter](https://solarquarter.com/2026/08/26/hperc-simplifies-rooftop-solar-approvals-under-pm-surya-ghar-scheme-in-himachal-pradesh/)

**In detail:** [HPERC Order](https://hperc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/FSolarroof4-26.pdf)

**Puducherry** increases **LPG (cooking gas) subsidy granted to red and yellow ration card holders by \$0.52 (INR 50).** Red and yellow card holders are some of the poorest in the country.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/puducherry-government-increases-lpg-subsidy-by-50-per-cylinder/article71403806.ece)

**Uttarakhand** approves the **Uttarakhand Motor Vehicles Taxation Reform Act, 2026.** The green tax exemption for Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) vehicles will continue, while the concessions for hybrid vehicles have been withdrawn.

**Source:** [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/business/2026/Aug/25/uttarakhand-clears-25-year-power-pact-with-adani-unveils-sweeping-policy-decisions)

**Finance**

Nothing critical to report this week.

**Industrial Policy and Business Regulations**

**Maharashtra** issues rules for the **Maharashtra Global Capability Center (GCC) Policy, 2025.** The operational guidelines translate the policy framework into a detailed procedure for application, approval and incentive claims, and appoint the Commissionerate of Services of the Maharashtra State as the implementing agency.

**Source:** [PWC](https://www.pwc.in/research-insights/news_alert/tax-insights/maharashtra-government-notifies-operational-guidelines-for-maharashtra-gcc-policy-2025.html)

**In detail:** [Operational Guidelines for Maharashtra Global Capability Centre (GCC) Policy, 2025](https://industry.maharashtra.gov.in/sites/default/files/2026-08/operational-guidelines-for-maharashtra-gcc-policy-2025.pdf)

**Uttar Pradesh** launches the **Uttar Pradesh Startup Policy, 2026.** The policy focuses on promoting emerging technologies such as Artificial Intelligence (AI), Quantum Computing, Blockchain, SpaceTech, DefenseTech, AgriTech, HealthTech, EdTech and other deep-tech domains.

**Source:** [DD News](https://ddnews.gov.in/en/adityanath-unveils-uttar-pradesh-startup-mission-portal-up-startup-policy-2026/)

**In detail:** [Uttar Pradesh Startup Policy-2026](https://invest.up.gov.in/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/UP-STARTUP-POLICY-EN_030826.pdf)

**Arunachal Pradesh** unveils the **Arunachal Human Capital and Economic Transformation Vision 2036 and the Arun Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME) Mission.** It outlines a long-term roadmap to strengthen skill development, entrepreneurship, and global employment opportunities for Arunachal Pradesh's youth.

**Source:** [India Today](https://www.indiatodayne.in/arunachal-pradesh/story/arunachal-pradesh-unveils-vision-2036-to-skill-1-lakh-youth-support-10000-entrepreneurs-1444145-2026-08-29)

**Puducherry** passes the **Puducherry Business Reforms Bill, 2026.** The bill streamlines regulatory procedures to reduce administrative delays and compliance burdens for businesses.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/cities/puducherry/assembly-passes-legislation-to-improve-business-environment-in-puducherry/article71389149.ece)

**In detail:** [Puducherry Business Reforms Bill, 2026](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:9a5e400e-5548-4352-a3bc-ed22755adced)

**Delhi** issues a new **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for issuing, renewing and inspecting cinematograph licenses for cinema halls and multiplexes.** It replaces the earlier system of separate No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from different departments with an integrated single-window process.

**Source:** [Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/cities/delhi/2026/Aug/18/delhi-govt-introduces-single-window-system-for-cinematograph-licences)

**Land Acquisition and Labor Regulations**

**Uttar Pradesh** approves the **Uttar Pradesh Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Rules, 2026.** Under the new rules, workers will be entitled to several facilities and protections, including appointment letters, an annual health check-up, canteen and rest-room facilities, as well as clearly defined rules for night shifts.

**Source:** [NDTV](https://www.ndtv.com/india-news/up-workers-to-get-appointment-letters-annual-health-checks-under-new-rules-11960742)

**In detail:** [Cabinet Decisions](https://information.up.gov.in/admin/UploadDocument/OtherPress/compressed_ce8f0109-428e-4e3e-b336-021544e0c8b7.pdf)

**Infrastructure and Governance**

**Maharashtra** publishes the draft **Maharashtra Private Coaching Centers (Registration and Regulation) Bill, 2026**. The bill seeks to establish a regulatory framework for private coaching centers, focusing on quality of education, student mental wellbeing, infrastructure and safety standards, fee regulation and grievance redressal mechanisms.

**Source:** [SCC Online](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2026/08/25/draft-maharashtra-private-coaching-centers-act-2026/)

**In detail:** [Maharashtra Private Coaching Centers (Registration and Regulation) Bill, 2026](https://education.maharashtra.gov.in/nfsshare/website_document/Notices_Uploads/2026.pdf)

**Tami Nadu** announces the **'Vetri Payanam' (Path to Success) Scheme.** It expands free bus travel for women and transgender commuters across Express, Limited Stop Service (LSS), and Deluxe government buses.

**Source:** [Economic Times](https://energy.economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/oil-and-gas/tamil-nadu-cm-vijay-announces-3-free-lpg-cylinders-expands-free-bus-travel-for-women-and-transgenders/133455787)

**In detail:** [Order](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:571beda9-9f96-497e-969d-1487c3e7defb)

**Uttar Pradesh** approves the '**Lokmata Ahilyabai Matrushakti Yojana' (Lokmata Ahilyabai Mother Power Scheme).** The scheme offers women aged 60 years and above lifelong free travel on all Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) buses.

**Source:** [The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/news/national/uttar-pradesh/uttar-pradesh-cabinet-approves-lifelong-free-bus-travel-for-women-aged-60-and-above/article71390009.ece)

**In detail:** [Cabinet Decisions](https://information.up.gov.in/admin/UploadDocument/OtherPress/compressed_ce8f0109-428e-4e3e-b336-021544e0c8b7.pdf)

**Uttar Pradesh** amends the **Uttar Pradesh Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Rules, 2014.** The new rules broaden the scope for elderly parents to effectively disinherit their children, for reasons such as abuse or lack of care.

**Source:** [The Indian Express](Uttar%20Pradesh%20Maintenance%20and%20Welfare%20of%20Parents%20and%20Senior%20Citizens%20Rules,%202014)

**In detail:** [Cabinet Decisions](https://information.up.gov.in/admin/UploadDocument/OtherPress/compressed_ce8f0109-428e-4e3e-b336-021544e0c8b7.pdf)

**Meghalaya** amends the **Meghalaya Identification, Registration (Safety and Security) of Migrant Workers Rules, 2020.** The new rules define the roles and powers of officials, inspection procedures, and applicable fines.

**Source:** [India Today](https://www.indiatodayne.in/meghalaya/story/meghalaya-to-scrap-interviews-for-non-technical-group-c-posts-from-2027-1443271-2026-08-27)

**Odisha** revises the rates of various financial assistance, under the **Odisha Rehabilitation and Resettlement Policy, 2006.** Under the revised rates, assistance for self-rehabilitation outside rehabilitation colonies has been increased from \$1,184 to \$1,219 (INR 1,13,128 to 1,16,550), while housing construction assistance has been enhanced from \$3,552 to \$3552 (INR 3,39,383 to 3,49,650).

**Source:** [New Indian Express](https://www.newindianexpress.com/states/odisha/2026/Aug/26/odisha-government-revises-fiscal-aid-under-2006-rehabilitation-and-resettlement-policy)

**In detail:** [Revised Rates](https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:VA6C2:da485e63-d976-4e2c-9893-0f67584bd2b2)
