**Maharashtra** publishes the draft **Maharashtra Private Coaching Centers (Registration and Regulation) Bill, 2026**. The bill seeks to establish a regulatory framework for private coaching centers, focusing on quality of education, student mental wellbeing, infrastructure and safety standards, fee regulation and grievance redressal mechanisms.
