**Haryana** amends **HERC (Electricity Supply Code) Regulations, 2014.** The regulations, issued by the Haryana Electricity Regulatory Commission (HERC), relax surcharge norms for excess contract demand. Consumers will no longer have to pay a surcharge if their power draw remains within 110 percent of the approved contract demand. Earlier, any draw beyond 105 percent attracted a 25 percent surcharge on total electricity charges.
