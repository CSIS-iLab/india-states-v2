**West Bengal** approves the **West Bengal Incentive Scheme (WBIS) 2026 for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)**. The scheme, in effect from October 1, 2026, will promote industrial growth, employment generation and balanced regional development. It will remain operational for 5 years until September 30, 2031.
