**Haryana** issues a policy for the **formalization of industrial colonies.** It will allow industrial establishments operating in unauthorized industrial clusters to be eligible for formalization, if they were constructed before October 3, 2025, and is spread over a minimum contiguous area of 10 acres and comprises at least 50 units.
