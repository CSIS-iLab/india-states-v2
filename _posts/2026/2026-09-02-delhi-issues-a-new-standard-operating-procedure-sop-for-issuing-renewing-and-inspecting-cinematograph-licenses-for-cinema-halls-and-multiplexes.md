**Delhi** issues a new **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) for issuing, renewing and inspecting cinematograph licenses for cinema halls and multiplexes.** It replaces the earlier system of separate No Objection Certificates (NOCs) from different departments with an integrated single-window process.
