**Delhi** approves a **Delhi Start-up and Incubation Policy**. Under this policy, more than \$41.42 million (INR 400 crore) will be invested over the next 5 years devoted to innovation, research, and entrepreneurship. In its primary phase, it will be implemented in 11 universities, 13 government-aided colleges, polytechnic institutions, Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) and government-run schools.
