**Himachal Pradesh** approves **HPERC Rooftop Solar PV Grid Interactive System (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2015**. The amendment, issued by the Himachal Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission (HPERC), aims to simplify the process for consumers installing rooftop solar systems in the state.
