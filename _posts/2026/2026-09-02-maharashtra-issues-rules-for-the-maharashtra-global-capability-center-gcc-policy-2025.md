**Maharashtra** issues rules for the **Maharashtra Global Capability Center (GCC) Policy, 2025.** The operational guidelines translate the policy framework into a detailed procedure for application, approval and incentive claims, and appoint the Commissionerate of Services of the Maharashtra State as the implementing agency.
