**Andhra Pradesh** releases draft **regulations for** **private hospitals, diagnostic centers, and clinics offering AYUSH (ayurveda, yoga, and naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and homeopathy) services.** As per the new regulations, registrations will be made mandatory for every private AYUSH facility.
