**Karnataka** passes the **Karnataka Municipal Corporations (Amendment) Bill, 2026 and the Karnataka Municipalities (Amendment) Bill, 2026.** The bills aim to provide a legal mechanism for dealing with roads and drains in unauthorized layouts and converting eligible properties from "B-Khata" (properties in unauthorized layouts) to "A-Khata" (properties that comply with all regulations). The bills apply to Municipal Corporations (for larger cities) and Municipalities (smaller cities and towns).
