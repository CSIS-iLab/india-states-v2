**Gujarat** launches a "**Scheme to Provide Assistance for Organ Transplants of Kidney, Liver, Heart, Pancreas, etc., and for Operations Including Robotic Surgeries for Patients Belonging to the Scheduled Tribe Community of the State**." The new welfare scheme for Scheduled Tribe (ST) patients suffering from serious illnesses comes with an allocation of \$0.2 million (INR 2 crore) for the Financial Year 2026-27.
