**Uttar Pradesh** approves the '**Lokmata Ahilyabai Matrushakti Yojana' (Lokmata Ahilyabai Mother Power Scheme).** The scheme offers women aged 60 years and above lifelong free travel on all Uttar Pradesh State Road Transport Corporation (UPSRTC) buses.
