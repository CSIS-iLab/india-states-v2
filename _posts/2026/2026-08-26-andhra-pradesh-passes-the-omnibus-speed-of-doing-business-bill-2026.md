**Andhra Pradesh** passes **The Omnibus (Speed of Doing Business) Bill, 2026**. It aims to simplify regulations, ease approval procedures and build a more investor-friendly, trust-based regulatory system, including an auto-appeal mechanism for delayed public services, revamped fire-safety audit rules, and a two-tier committee system to fast-track industrial clearances.
