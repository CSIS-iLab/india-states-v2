**Uttar Pradesh** issues **Draft UPERC (Uttar Pradesh Electricity Grid Code) Regulations, 2026**. The regulations, issued by the Uttar Pradesh Electricity Regulatory Commission, intend to modernize the operational framework for intra-state transmission systems, improve grid security, and enhance the overall power system management.
