**Andhra Pradesh** launches '**Netannala Sevalo' (Serving Weavers) Scheme.** As per the scheme, below-poverty-line handloom weaver families will receive annual financial assistance of \$259.13 (INR 25,000), irrespective of the beneficiary's age or the number of looms owned.
