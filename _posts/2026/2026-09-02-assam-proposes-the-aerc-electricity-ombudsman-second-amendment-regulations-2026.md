**Assam** proposes the **AERC (Electricity Ombudsman) (Second Amendment) Regulations, 2026**. The amendment seeks to modify the Assam Electricity Regulatory Commission (AERC) (Electricity Ombudsman) Regulations, 2022, particularly the provisions governing the pay, allowances, and other benefits of the Electricity Ombudsman.
