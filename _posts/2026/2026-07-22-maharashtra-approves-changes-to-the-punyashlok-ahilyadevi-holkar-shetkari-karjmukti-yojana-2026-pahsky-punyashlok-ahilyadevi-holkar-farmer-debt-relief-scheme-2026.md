**Maharashtra** approves changes to the '**Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Shetkari Karjmukti Yojana 2026 (PAHSKY)' (Punyashlok Ahilyadevi Holkar Farmer Debt Relief Scheme 2026)**. It removes key eligibility conditions, including the previous \$517.83 (INR 50,000) upper limit applicable to the beneficiaries of the 2019 waiver. It also scraps the condition of regular loan repayment during 2026-27, making nearly 2.3 million farmers eligible for incentive benefits under the scheme.
