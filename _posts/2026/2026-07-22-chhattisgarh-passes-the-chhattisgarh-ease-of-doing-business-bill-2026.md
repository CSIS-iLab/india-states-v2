**Chhattisgarh** passes the **Chhattisgarh Ease of Doing Business Bill, 2026.** The legislation adopts a statutory risk-based approval system aimed at reducing bureaucratic delays, cutting compliance costs, and accelerating investments, particularly for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs).
