**Delhi** announces a phased shift towards **cleaner light goods vehicles (LGVs).** Under the new policy, only electric N1 light goods vehicles (with a maximum mass not exceeding 7,716 pounds/ or 3.5 tons) will be allowed to be registered in Delhi from January 1, 2027. In the wider National Capital Region (NCR), the rule will be enforced from July 1, 2027.
