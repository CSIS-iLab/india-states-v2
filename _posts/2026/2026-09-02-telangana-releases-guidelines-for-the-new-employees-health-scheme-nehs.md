**Telangana** releases guidelines for the **New Employees Health Scheme (NEHS).** The guidelines raise the cashless medical coverage limit from \$2,093 to \$3,140 (INR 2 lakh to 3 lakh) per episode of illness, with no limit on the total number of episodes.
