**West Bengal** announces **restrictions on the powers of municipalities and other local bodies**. Elected representatives, including municipal chairpersons and 'Gram Panchayat' (Village Council) heads, no longer have the authority to issue birth and death certificates. Only designated government officials would be authorized to issue birth and death certificates.
