**Maharashtra** abolishes the **one-year Social Responsibility Service required of Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) graduates from government, municipal, government-aided, and private unaided medical colleges.** It does not apply to postgraduate and super-speciality degree recipients.
