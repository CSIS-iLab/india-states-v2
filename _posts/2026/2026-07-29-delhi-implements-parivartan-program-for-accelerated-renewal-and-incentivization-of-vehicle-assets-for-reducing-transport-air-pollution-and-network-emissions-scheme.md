**Delhi** implements **PARIVARTAN - Program for Accelerated Renewal and Incentivization of Vehicle Assets for Reducing Transport Air Pollution and Network Emissions Scheme**. The scheme aims to reduce air pollution by incentivizing the replacement of older polluting trucks and buses in the National Capital Region (NCR).
